# Operations

## Normal operation

Deployments poll every 60 seconds and verify the Ed25519 signature, key ID, account, deployment audience, issued-at time, and expiry. A verified cache may bridge a control-plane outage for six hours. With enforcement on, no valid live or cached token means denied access.

## Suspension

Block before any user response or conversational-state mutation. Do not delete or rewrite WhatsApp sessions, Telegram sessions, QR data, bot configuration, panel data, or persistent volumes. Do not disclose the billing reason to end users.

## Recovery

A valid recurrent paid webhook or a final, correlated one-time order returns the account to `active`. Both deployments refresh without restarts and should reactivate within two minutes. Operators may use a reasoned override only as a bounded incident action.

Do not switch from a live recurring agreement to PSE/crypto by creating a second charge. Cancel/expire the recurring relationship first. Only one one-time order may remain pending, and questionable provider events go to reconciliation without granting access.

## Rollback

Set `BILLING_ENFORCEMENT=false` after explicit deployment authorization. This disables blocking without deleting the payment history or signed cache. Never make suspension depend on a calendar event.
